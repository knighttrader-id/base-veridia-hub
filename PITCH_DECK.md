## 💡 **Slide 3: The Solution**

### **VeridiaHub - Web3 Copyright Platform**

**🔗 On-Chain Copyright Recording**
- SHA-256 hash-based immutable copyright registration
- ERC-721 NFT representation of creative works
- Permanent, tamper-proof ownership records

**🎫 Advanced License Management**
- ERC-1155 semi-fungible license tokens
- Time-based expiration and renewal
- Automated royalty distribution (95% creator, 4% platform, 1% national fund)

**⚡ Gas-Optimized Operations**
- 20-33% gas savings through batch operations
- Unlimited scalability with O(1) creator queries
- Base Layer-2 for low-cost, high-throughput transactions
